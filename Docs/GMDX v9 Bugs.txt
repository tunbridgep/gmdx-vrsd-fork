I recommend against playing v9 if you can avoid it, it has some very serious
bugs and issues, some of which are fixed in vRSD and almost all of which are
fixed in AE.

Major Bugs (gamebreaking issues or severe exploits):

- The piercing perk is completely useless because the damage is modified after
  it's applied (Fixed in AE). Several other perks are assigned incorrectly and
  give the wrong effects due to misnumbered perkNamesArray checks (Mostly fixed
  in vRSD, fully fixed in AE because it overhauls the perk system entirely)
- Lots of renderer related crashes, including black-screen CTDs because of
  renderer overflows, the most egregious examples being near the big boat in HK
  Canals and one of the stations near a crashed train in Brooklyn Bridge
  Station (Fixed in AE and DXRando)
- The EUAS hazard detection has a roughly 10% chance of causing a CTD every
  time a thrown enemy grenade detonates because of accessed nones (partially
  fixed in vRSD, fully fixed in AE)
- The v9 "fix" for Paul dying before setting off the 'Ton raid means that if he
  dies AT ALL during the raid, as long as you survive and make it out of the
  hotel, he will be alive in the next mission. You can even take his corpse
  with you into the prison level and he will still be alive, resulting in 2
  instances of Paul Denton. (Fixed in AE)
- The added "item physics" blocks spawning in some cases, resulting in some
  messed-up bugs, such as picking up grenades with 0 ammo occasionally, or
  blowing up enemies and having them drop no items because their gibs are
  overlapping with their item spawns. This is especially noticeable in vRSD
  because it added a gas grenade to a cupboard in the first UNATCO HQ mission,
  which will always give you 0 ammo if it's the first gas grenade you pickup on
  a given playthrough because it doesn't have room to spawn the ammo. (Fixed in
  AE, all items now spawn ammo correctly always)
- When being transferred from the 'Ton Hotel raid to the MJ12 Prison map by
  dying, you will reasonably often randomly lose a handful of inventory items
  (usually items towards the end of the inventory list) because GMDX clears
  your inventory when you die (to fix the self-frob inventory duplication
  exploit) before transferring you to the next map. This most often results in
  losing the aug canister from the previous mission, because it's usually the
  last item in your inventory. (Fixed in AE)
- Left-Frobbing a stack of items (such as multiple biocells in the armoury
  after being imprisoned by MJ12) will delete the entire stack rather than
  using 1 from it (Fixed in AE)
- Disabling a camera or turret with an EMP, then disabling it via hacking or
  multitools, will cause it to wake-up and become active again after the EMP
  effect ends (Fixed in AE)
- Environmental Training items have the same durations/protections at Trained
  and Advanced, due to rounding errors, so the only benefit is the extra
  pickups (Fixed in AE)
- Ammo can be duplicated by frobbing corpses with a full inventory, then
  dropping items until you have space to pick up their weapon and re-frobbing,
  which can even be used to duplicate LAMs and GEP ammo! (Fixed in AE)
- Ammo can be duplicated by frobbing corpses, then gibbing them, which would
  refill their weapon (especially problematic for GEP guns). (Fixed in AE)
- The ADS augmentation can trigger invisible grenade traps, creating gas clouds
  in Versalife and other places. (Fixed in AE)
- Jumping off a ladder while partially submerged in water (such as the ladder
  at the start of the Battery Park mission) will cause JC to enter a permanent
  flying state. (Fixed in AE)
- Cameras do not properly set off alarm events when spotting carcasses. This
  means, for instance, that on the Dockyards mission, being caught by a camera
  and setting off an alarm will release the bots from the armoury, but cameras
  spotting carcasses will set off the alarm without releaseing the bots (Fixed
  in AE, camera behaviour is now consistent)
- Electronics Panels that are destroyed by hitting their DT exactly won't
  trigger their associated event due to it checking for damage above the DT
  only (fixed in AE) [I can't remember if this was a vRSD or v9 feature]
- Using the GEP Scope while up against a wall will allow you to see through it,
  providing free wallhacks. Worse, this can crash the game as it may cause the
  renderer overflow mentioned above. (Fixed in AE)
- You can use the inventory freely while paused by using the PAUSE key,
  circumventing "real time UI" restrictions entirely (Fixed in AE)

Minor Bugs (visual bugs or annoyances):

- Alternate Toolbelt resetting on map transition, losing your primary selection
  and making the feature somewhat useless (Fixed in AE)
- Knocking scuba-divers unconscious will immediately kill them the moment they
  float to the surface, ruining any non-lethal gameplay against them (Fixed in
  AE)
- The "Create Custom Theme" menu can affect the default themes, changing them
  until the game is restarted and not saving the changes as a result. (Fixed in
  AE by always forcing you into the Custom theme when using the custom theme
  creation menu)
- If Subtitles are disabled, an empty subtitles box will appear every time you
  interact with an object during a first-person conversation. (Fixed in AE)
- The "Crosshair Visibility" setting in the menu is constantly overwritten
  whenever you use a laser, making the setting completely useless (Fixed in AE)
- Subtitles don't show in the intro cutscene due to bad aspect-ratio checking
  (Fixed in AE)
- GMDX's Stamina "tiredness" (slow melee swings, etc) works on a timer, so you
  could make it end prematurely by running out of stamina while the effect is
  active, which would prematurely end the second effect (Fixed in AE)
- The Aggressive Defense System aug will show all wall-mounted grendes at
  infinite range, and will cause endless beeping and earrape. (Partially fixed
  in vRSD, fully fixed in AE)
- Framerate-dependent scoping-in animation timings (and they are disabled
  entirely on DX9), making the "faster scope time" perks in vRSD mostly
  worthless and causing other issues. This affects the "weapon pull back" when
  near walls animation too, but that's purely visual (Fixed in AE, now it's
  always timing-consistent and works on all renderers)
- Due to a hacky fix for the "We have a deal" conversation bug, ALL mechanics
  bail out of trade-related conversations early, resulting in missing out on
  some dialog lines, most notably the mechanic in the Airfield Terminal. (Fixed
  in AE)
- Grenade Traps (when you're in vents) are half-broken and only ever work once,
  often don't trigger at all, and are exclusively limited to gas grenades
  (Fixed in AE)
- When activating the spy drone aug, if you're looking at a wall, the drone
  will not be created, but the aug will still be active and will drain energy
  until switched off. (Fixed in AE, now it won't activate at all if it can't
  create the drone)
- Several items don't have fixed hand skins (notably POVCorpses), so they don't
  change colour to reflect the players skin colour similarly to how other items
  do (Fixed in AE)
- Due to changes to the way carcasses are detected in v9, rat carcasses can set
  off some enemies (most notably in the Paris Club entrance, killing rats will
  turn the guards hostile) (Fixed in AE)
- Double-Click Holstering uses a Timer, which will cause slowdowns and FPS loss
  over time as more timers are created and destroyed due to an Unreal engine
  issue (Replaced with a static game time check in AE)
- Carcasses show the name of the entity or creature when killed (such as "Dead
  Body (Terrorist)"), but carcasses placed in the map simply show as "Dead
  Body" (Fixed in AE)
- Opening the main menu while dead will re-enable the HUD (Fixed in AE)
- MJ12 Elites can sometimes be permanently cloaked when revisiting them after
  transitioning maps (partially fixed in vRSD, fully fixed in AE and made into
  a modifier)
- Combat Strength affects the damage of some non hand-to-hand weapons which are
  marked as hand-to-hand in the engine (such as the PS20) (Fixed in AE, now
  it's affected by Targeting)
- Using Cloak and Radar Trans will completely fuck up rendering of some
  weapons/items (such as the nanokey) until one or both of them is switched off
  (Fixed in vRSD)
- Searching carcasses for items where we would go above a full stack deletes
  the extra items (fixed in AE, now it only takes what it can)
- Extremely inconsistent enemy behaviour due to NPCs being modified directly in
  the maps (Some elites have a lot less health than others because they are
  actually renamed and reskinned MJ12 Troops, some MJ12Commandos have the wrong
  footstep sounds due to them being set in the map files directly, dafoe's
  quest for "killing 3 people in the metro" only actually has 2 targets because
  some Commandos were set to bImportant, etc). MJ12 Elites are immune to tear
  gas, MJ12 Troops aren't (including ones that have been reskinned as Elites),
  resulting in inconsistent behaviour in the maps as well. This is fully fixed
  in AE by splitting out the weird versions of the NPCs into subclasses and
  making sure all code paths work for all of them at all times, and replacing
  the highly-modified NPCs in the maps with the new subclassed versions.
- Hacking window doesn't ever show at Untrained hacking, making it impossible
  to use Nuke viruses without at least 1 level in hacking (Fixed in AE)
- The "Confirm note deletion" checkbox does nothing on Hardcore mode or with
  realtime UI turned on, notes are always deleted instantly (Fixed in AE)
- Buying a weapon with attachments (such as Smugglers autoshotgun) won't add
  those attachments to your existing copy if you already have the weapon (Fixed
  in AE, not that anyone buys his autoshotty anyway)
- Muscle aug doesn't prevent jumping while carrying heavy objects, due to the
  check being in the wrong place (Fixed in AE)
- Some items will have empty name strings (Partially fixed in vRSD, fully fixed
  in AE)
- GMDX v9 darkened all the decorative items in the maps for aesthetics. However
  they are not reset properly when being picked up, so dropped items like
  couches will be brighter after being dropped once. (Fixed in AE)

There are also several major Vanilla issues that are left unfixed in v9 and
vRSD, some of which are quite major:

- Thermoptic camo isn't effective against activated turrets (but still works
  against cameras and bots) due to a missing conditional check (Fixed in AE)
- Items can be duplicated by picking them up multiple times within 1-2 frames
  (such as by using the mousewheel), which is a favourite tactic of
  speedrunners (Fixed in AE)
- Glasses frequently appear on holograms, and usually look awful (solid white)
  (Fixed in AE)
- Patrolling enemies that get stuck (most often happens in the ShipBelow map)
  will cause an infinite loop and crash the game (Fixed in AE)
- The game would try to initiate conversations sometimes when one of the
  participants was far away (such as talking to Renault in Paris while his
  bodyguard is far away), which would momentarily throw the player into the
  Conversation state (preventing movement) for a fraction of a second before
  bailing. (Fixed in AE, now it bails correctly)

vRSD also has it's fair share of major issues, the primary one being that
Alcohol still gives fullness with the addiction system enabled, but doesn't
check for fullness when consumed, so you can get past 100% fullness, and
allowing you to use charged pickups (namely tech goggles) for a split second
when they are empty if they are in the secondary slot.
